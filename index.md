## Selected work

- [TweetyBERT](https://github.com/georgevenven/tweety_bert) learns to segment and group canary song syllables from unlabeled recordings.
- [SongMAE](https://github.com/georgevenven/SongMAE) learns representations of birdsong by reconstructing masked parts of spectrograms.
- [Birdsong detection](https://github.com/georgevenven/birdsong-detect-distill) adapts SongMAE to find song in audio labeled by a vision-language model.
